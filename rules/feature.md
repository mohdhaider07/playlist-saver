# MyTaalim Core Features

- **YouTube Course & Video Ingestion**: Import full YouTube playlists or individual videos into a focused, distraction-free player with automated metadata & durations.
- **Custom Playlist Builder**: Add additional videos to any single-video imported playlist directly from the player sidebar and rename playlists anytime.
- **Progress Tracking & Status Lifecycle**: Automatically track video watch duration and timestamps, with playlist statuses (`in_progress` / `completed`) toggled via card actions.
- **Smart Filtering & Bilingual Support**: Filter dashboard courses by status (defaults to in-progress) and channel, fully supported in English and Arabic (RTL).
